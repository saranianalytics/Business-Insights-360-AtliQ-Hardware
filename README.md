# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Power BI Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360 (BI 360)** is an end-to-end **Power BI Business Intelligence solution** developed for *AtliQ Hardware*, a global **B2B hardware manufacturing company**.
The company operates across **Brick & Mortar and E-commerce platforms** and serves **retailers, distributors, and e-commerce partners** across multiple regions, including Latin America.

As the business expanded, AtliQ Hardware faced a **decline in net profit despite stable or growing net sales**. This was driven by **rising operational expenses, inventory inefficiencies, and limited cross-functional visibility**. Existing Excel-based reporting was manual, time-consuming, and not scalable for effective decision-making.

This project addresses those challenges by building a **centralized BI dashboard** that provides a 360° view of business performance.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

* Declining net profit despite healthy net sales
* Heavy dependency on Excel-based manual reporting
* Poor visibility into cost drivers, inventory risks, and regional performance
* Difficulty in making timely, data-driven decisions due to scattered data

---

## 💡 Solution Approach

A centralized **Power BI dashboard (BI 360)** was developed to integrate data across multiple business functions.
The dashboard acts as a **single source of truth** and enables real-time insights for stakeholders at different levels.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Views

The BI 360 solution includes the following views:

* **Finance View**
  Analyzes Net Sales, Operating Expenses, and Net Profit trends with year-over-year comparisons to identify declining profitability and cost drivers.

* **Sales View**
  Evaluates customer, product, and channel performance to identify revenue concentration and low-margin customers.

* **Marketing View**
  Assesses product and segment performance using performance matrices to support pricing and portfolio decisions.

* **Supply Chain View**
  Tracks forecast accuracy, excess inventory, and out-of-stock risks, linking operational inefficiencies to financial impact.

* **Executive View**
  Provides a high-level KPI snapshot for leadership to enable faster, data-driven strategic decisions.
